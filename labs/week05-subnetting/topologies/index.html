Export w05-a2-asymmetric

The addressing has to be exact for the activity to work: .11 with /24 must fall outside the /25, and .200 with /25 must fall inside it. Students who pick their own numbers often choose two that land in the same half, and then nothing happens and the activity appears broken.

Export it with the fault already in place, including send_redirects=0 on R1 — with redirects on, H2 learns a host route after the first ping and the asymmetry vanishes mid-activity.

A5: export the shell, never the addressing

w05-a5-design should contain the nodes and cabling only — one OL9 Router, four hosts, four Ethernet switches, no addresses anywhere.

Choosing the addressing is the entire assignment. An export with any address in it hands out the answer, including to the students who would have got there themselves.

A4 is deliberately not exported

Calculating four /26 boundaries and then discovering an off-by-one when a host cannot reach its own gateway is the activity. A pre-built version removes the feedback loop that makes this week work at all.
